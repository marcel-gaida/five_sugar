# Company Rating Lookup

An advanced Chrome Extension that allows you to instantly check Glassdoor employee ratings and Google Maps business ratings for any company.

## Features
- **Context Menu Lookup**: Highlight any company name on the web, right-click, and select "Look up company ratings" to view its ratings in a sleek floating popup.
- **LinkedIn Jobs Scanner**: Instantly batch-scan an entire page of LinkedIn job postings to fetch ratings for all listed companies.
- **Indeed Jobs Scanner**: Seamlessly scan Indeed job listings to reveal the ratings of all hiring companies directly on the page.
- **Smart Result Caching**: Saves scanned results during your session to minimize API calls and prevent layout shifts.

## Setup & Installation
[⬇️ Download v1.1.6](https://github.com/marcel-gaida/five_sugar/blob/088b0a407c4d3c7a2ce26a1b2bd0902199a7250c/company-rating-lookup-1.1.6/company-rating-lookup-1.1.6.zip) and unpack.
1. Go to `chrome://extensions/` in your Chrome browser.
2. Turn on **Developer mode** (toggle switch in the top right corner).
3. Click **Load unpacked**.
4. Select the `company-rating-lookup-1.1.0` folder.
5. The extension is now installed! You can configure optional Google Places API settings by clicking the extension icon.

## Usage
- **Highlight & Right-Click**: Simply highlight any text on any webpage, right-click, and select "Look up company ratings".
- **LinkedIn / Indeed Scanning**: When browsing LinkedIn Jobs or Indeed, click the extension icon in your toolbar and select "LinkedIn" or "Indeed" to open the interactive scanner panel. Click "Scan" to inject inline ratings on the page.
